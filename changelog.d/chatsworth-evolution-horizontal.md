### Added
- The Chatsworth Products Evolution horizontal cable managers, thirteen devices under the new
  `chatsworth` vendor: `35441-x01` (1U solid), `35441-x02`, `-x03` and `-x04` (2U to 4U with
  cable pass-through ports), `35442-x02`, `-x03` and `-x04` (solid), `33548-x02`, `-x03` and
  `-x04` (with brushes) and `33549-x02`, `-x03` and `-x04` (with grommets). Each is a
  `rack-face` sheet part 482.6 x 208 mm, with a black and a Glacier White configuration that
  carry the part numbers (`35441-702` and `35441-E02`, and so on), ten T-shaped fingers a row
  top and bottom, a `duct` guide, a snap-on door that is its own removable part, the `passes`
  its chassis style has, and the maker's cable fill for Cat 6 (#1093, part of #1091). The
  fingers, ports, brush and grommets are read off pictures and are `estimated`. A grommet and
  a door that swings up or down cannot be stated in the format and are gaps. The Evolution G3
  verticals, Motive, Velocity and the Universal manager are left for later.
