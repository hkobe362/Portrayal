### Added
- **`@portrayal/kit` is 0.29.0: a cable has a colour of its own, and its
  ends and its type can give it one** (#1028). A cable was drawn in the
  colour of its media and nothing could change it. It is now drawn in the
  first of these that says something: the colour stated on the cable
  (`color` in the rack file); else the jacket colour of its two seated ends,
  when both state one and the two agree; else the `jacket` of the cable type
  it names; else its media's, as before. **No default changed:** every media
  is the colour it was (an AOC is still `#3a3f45`), so a rack that states no
  colour is drawn exactly as it was, and no recorded answer of the kit
  moves. `cableColor(link)` in `rack/cable-geometry.js` gives `{color,
  from}` and `colorOf(link)` the colour; `cablePlan(rack, readers)` and
  `cablePlan3d(rack, readers)` put what decides it on each link (`color`,
  `jackets`, `typeColor`, each only when it says something).
- **`color` is a new saved key of a cable**, with no new rack file version:
  a reader keeps a cable key it does not know. It is `#` and six lower-case
  hex digits; the reader takes `#rgb` and either case and writes it so. A
  `color` that is no colour moves, whole, to `colorAsWritten`, and the cable
  is drawn by its ends, its type or its media, never in black. **A hand
  edit that put some other value under `color`** (a name, a number) was kept
  there as an unknown key by a kit before this one, and is now moved.
  `rack.schema.json` under `/schemas/v3/` describes both keys; `color` has a
  `pattern`, the first in that schema, and `rack/validate.js` now knows the
  keyword.
- **`cable.add` and `cable.update` take `color`.** `null` or `""` takes the
  cable's own colour off. A value that is no colour is refused: "A cable
  color is # and three or six hex digits, such as #2bb3c8, or empty for none
  of its own." `parseColor(text)` in `rack/cable-rules.js` is the same rule
  for a form.
- **What an end states** is read by `seatedJacket(item, end, ctx)` in
  `rack/ports.js`, from the parts lists `loadSlots` returns. The declared
  default of a `jacket-color` field is not a statement (it would turn every
  cable between two generic ends black): an end states a colour when the
  rack sets the field on the part seated in that port, or when the part's
  own contract gives its jacket one. `jacketAt` and `jacketsFor` lay a
  page's own reading over that: a `jacket` key on an end's entry of its
  cable facts.
- `colorFacts`, `cableColorOf` and `colorsKey` in `rack/cable-rules.js`;
  `hexColor`, `agreedJacket`, `mediaColor` and `bundleColor` in
  `rack/cable-geometry.js`; `jacketLookup` in `rack/cable-types.js`, and
  `typeColorOf` in what `loadCableTypes` returns. `sameCables3d` tells a
  change of a cable's own colour.
- **draw.io:** a row of `drawioCables` carries `color` when the cable's
  colour is said (its own, its ends' or its type's), and `kit/drawio.js`
  draws a cable that carries one in it, ahead of the `cableStyle` map. A
  cable coloured by its media alone is written as before. `drawioCables`
  takes `typeColorOf` in its options.
- **NetBox and Nautobot:** the `color` column both cables files already had
  now holds the cable's own colour, six lower-case hex digits with no `#`,
  and stays blank for every other cable. Both store a cable's colour so
  (NetBox v4.7.2 251458b8, `dcim/models/cables.py` and
  `utilities/validators.py`; Nautobot v3.2.6 3dc554b4,
  `dcim/models/cables.py` and `core/models/fields.py`). Not tried on a
  running instance.
- `inspect` of a cable has `color`, and `describe` lists it after the media,
  for a cable that states one.
- **`cable-types.json` is version 1.1.0.** A type may carry `jacket`:
  `{color, basis, sources, note}`, `basis` being `standard`, `convention` or
  `photo`. The build refuses one on a bare media type, so the kit's `COLORS`
  stays the one table of the colours a media is drawn in. The new type
  `aoc-siemon-q1q28f` (Siemon's 100G QSFP28 AOC: 3.0 mm, a 30 mm minimum
  bend radius, and an aqua jacket counted on its sheet's photograph) is the
  first with one. No rack names it, so nothing is recoloured.

### Changed
- `JACKETS` in `rack/cable-geometry.js` names entries of `COLORS` and holds
  no colour of its own. Its four values are what they were. **Open:**
  `COLORS` draws an AOC dark grey, the front-page hero draws one aqua with
  the `mm` jacket, and `CABLE_COLOURS` in `kit/drawio.js` draws one blue.
  None of the three was changed; `docs/rack-file-v4-design.md` section 14.5
  states the question.
