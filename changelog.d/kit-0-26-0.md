### Changed
- **`@portrayal/kit` is 0.26.0. Routed lengths on saved racks move (#1026,
  #987).** The automatic route chooses a lead's ring by room, not by
  distance: the first ring on its way that it can turn into within its
  cable's installed bend radius, a ring too close being passed over; a patch
  along one manager with no ring between its ports takes the ring it lays
  better through; and a detour round a tray keeps its turns their room, or
  comes up behind the tray where a device is set back. So a cable whose
  automatic route or detour changes measures differently the next time a
  page measures it. On the sample of 60 generated racks 340 of 1,803 cables
  move, by -340 to +125 mm (194 shorter, 146 longer), 5 go up a stock size
  and 31 down; on twelve racks of thirteen 2U switches with a lacer on each,
  648 of 3,300, by -575 to +327 mm, 18 up and 78 down; a rack with no
  manager is as it was. An entered length and a route made by hand are never
  touched. Corners short of a cable's bend radius fall from 955 to 422 on
  the sample.
- Two turns on one short leg are one `tight-bend` finding, with `turns` and
  `apart_mm`, where they were two; a finding at a lead that no ring leaves
  room says the manager is too close and what would help (`tooClose`). The
  kit README lists what a page reads under "New in 0.26.0". No rack file,
  column or type file changes.
