### Changed
- **`@portrayal/kit` is 0.25.0: the rack kit is faster on a large rack, and
  gives the same answers.** No path, length, need, stock size, finding,
  sentence, strap, spare figure or export row changes, and no name is
  removed or renamed. On a generated rack of thirteen two-unit switches, a
  lacer on each, two ducts and 300 cords: a first read of every path
  36 s to 0.6 s (about 60 times); a second read 2.2 s to 3 ms; a
  read in a context made anew 36 s to 26 ms; a read after one cable
  moved 36 s to 26 ms; after one device moved 36 s to 0.6 s; each
  rack-wide call (`bendFindings`, `coverFindings`, `ringFindings`,
  `bodyFindings`, `fill`, `capacityOver`, `spareOf`, `anchorsOf`) about
  2.2 s to 3 ms or less; `spareExports` 13 s to 3 ms; `describe`
  7 s to 18 ms. The lay of a cable is kept, by what it read, so a
  context made anew or an edit to another cable lays nothing again; a device
  placed, moved or removed still lays every cable again. `routePath` hands
  out a copy of the path kept. `spareOf` takes the path as an optional
  fourth argument. A context changed in place gives the path laid before
  the change, whole, where it gave a mixture; a context was already to be
  made whole and not changed. The kit README says what is kept and what
  lays a cable again, under "New in 0.25.0";
  `spec/tests/js/rack-speed.mjs` holds every answer for 91 racks and
  3,911 cables to the one 0.24.0 gave, and holds the budgets.
