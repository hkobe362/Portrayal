### Changed
- **`@portrayal/kit` is 0.27.0. Routed lengths on saved racks move (#1026,
  #987).** The automatic route chooses a lead's ring by room, not by
  distance: the first ring on its way that it can turn into within its
  cable's installed bend radius, a ring too close being passed over; a patch
  along one manager with no ring between its ports takes the ring it lays
  better through; and a detour round a tray keeps its turns their room, or
  comes up behind the tray where a device is set back. So a cable whose
  automatic route or detour changes measures differently the next time a
  page measures it. On the sample of 60 generated racks 332 of 1,803 cables
  move, by -340 to +121 mm (187 shorter, 145 longer), 4 go up a stock size
  and 30 down; on twelve racks of thirteen 2U switches with a lacer on each,
  639 of 3,300, by -575 to +61 mm, 10 up and 77 down; those of them with
  no lacer are as they were. An entered length is never touched, and a
  route made by hand keeps its waypoints (the lay between them is the
  kit's, and can move); what is read off a routed length moves with it (the cable
  schedule, the BOM's cord sizes, the spare, a sheet, a DCIM cables file's
  lengths), and a cord already bought at the old size can read as too short
  where its path grew (none of 7,210 measured does). No cable changes
  gutter. Corners short of a cable's bend radius fall from 955 to 422 on
  the sample.
- Two turns on one short leg are one `tight-bend` finding, with `turns` and
  `apart_mm`, where they were two; a finding at a lead that no ring leaves
  room says the manager is too close and what would help (`tooClose`, and
  `advice`, a sentence of its own, which adds nothing to the finding's
  `text`). A pair's sentence is longer than any bend's was (up to 182
  characters, from 150), so a label clipped at a fixed length is cut more
  often. A first read of a large rack takes about half as long again as in
  0.26.0, and so does a read after a device is moved. The
  kit README lists what a page reads under "New in 0.27.0". No rack file,
  column or type file changes.
