### Added
- Leviton horizontal cable managers (#1100, part of #1091), modelled from the maker's own
  dimensional drawings, with `leviton` added to the vendor registry (listed after `apc`). The
  five 49253 five-ring managers: `leviton/49253-lpm` (1RU, 4 in rings), `49253-bcm` (2RU, 4
  in), `49253-2cm` (2RU, 2 in), `49253-6pm` (1RU, 6 in) and `49253-6mr` (2RU, 6 in). Each has
  five `ring` guides with the drawn opening and a slit in each ring's front end, and a
  `with-cover` configuration that fits the separately sold metal cover, `49253-1cv` (1RU) or
  `49253-2cv` (2RU), which comes off. The three VERSI-DUCT slotted ducts: `leviton/491ru-hfr`
  (1RU, front and rear), `492ru-hfr` (2RU, front and rear) and `492ru-hfo` (2RU, front only).
  Each has a `duct` guide per channel with the drawn finger pitch (49.375 mm) and slot gap
  (22.45 mm), a snap-on cover that ships fitted and comes off in a `cover-off` configuration,
  and, on the two front-and-rear ducts, four oval pass-throughs in the top and four in the
  bottom. Cable capacity is the figure Leviton's application note states, with its basis. The
  designer covers 49265-DC1 and 49265-DC2 are not modelled: Leviton gives their width and
  profile depth but no height.
